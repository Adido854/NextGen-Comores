This is intelligencia school in Comoros . In my own city Anjouan. I realized that English is an ever important language for students. It is a golden language which can open opportunities worldwide for students. With my then boyfriend who became my husbands , we decided that something need to be done about it , to increase english interest among students in Comoros. That led us to the first book entitled "Langue en Or" which definitly became my signature in the island with more than 5,000 copies sold.
